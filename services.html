FAQ

Service FAQs

Can I mix modes?

Yes. Multimodal shipments combining road, rail, sea and air are planned and tracked as one booking with a single set of documents.

Is there an API?

A fully documented REST API and webhooks are included in every plan, along with sandbox keys and SDKs for common languages.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is usage-based per shipment with volume discounts, so you only pay for the services you use. Enterprise plans can be tailored to committed volumes.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams go live within two to four weeks, including carrier connections, data migration and staff training delivered by a dedicated implementation specialist.

Do you offer SLAs?

Yes. Every paid plan includes a 99.99% uptime SLA, with a guaranteed 15-minute response time on priority support.
