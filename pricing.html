Billing FAQs

Can I cancel?

You can cancel at any time from your account settings. There are no penalties, and your data can be exported in CSV or JSON.

Free trial?

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial of all features. No credit card is required and you can upgrade when you are ready.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, bank transfers and invoicing with net-30 terms for enterprise plans.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes. Choose annual billing to save 20% compared to paying monthly, with the option to switch at renewal.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and your charges are pro-rated from the day you switch.
