Solution FAQs

Custom workflows?

Approval rules, document templates, notifications and compliance checks can all be configured per industry, customer or lane without writing code.

Integrations?

Ready-made connectors exist for SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, Shopify and Magento, plus EDI for large trading partners.
