FAQ

Common questions

Where are you based?

Our headquarters is in Chennai, India, with regional offices in Singapore, Dubai and London so that customers receive support in their own time zone.

Do you offer support 24/7?

Yes. Live chat, phone and email support are available around the clock, with a guaranteed 15-minute response on priority plans.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams go live within two to four weeks. A dedicated implementation specialist helps with data migration, carrier connections and staff training.

Does Stackly integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We provide open APIs and pre-built connectors for popular ERP, WMS, OMS and eCommerce platforms, so Stackly fits into the tools you already use.

Is my logistics data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in ISO 27001-certified facilities, with role-based access controls and regular independent security audits.
